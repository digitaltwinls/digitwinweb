Principles That Guide Our Work

Human-centred

Technology should strengthen learning relationships and decisions, not replace the people at their centre.

Responsible

Privacy, transparency, fairness and appropriate governance are considered from the beginning.

Evidence-led

Recommendations are connected to defined outcomes, available evidence and measurable value.
